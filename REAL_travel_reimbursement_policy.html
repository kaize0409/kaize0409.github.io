Student Travel Reimbursement Policy

REAL-AI Lab  |  Effective August 2026
Purpose. This policy is intended to make effective and responsible use of the lab’s limited travel budget, avoid unnecessary expenses, and ensure that available funds can support as many students as possible. The amounts below are maximum reimbursement caps, not fixed allowances. The lab reimburses only actual, allowable, documented expenses; any unused portion of a cap will not be paid or carried forward. All reimbursements remain subject to applicable University rules and funding restrictions.

1. University Travel Support Must Be Used First

Students must use their available University travel support before requesting travel reimbursement from the lab. These support amounts are maximum caps, and reimbursement is based on actual, allowable, documented expenses.

  • PhD students: Each PhD student has two University travel-support allocations of up to $2,000 each, consisting of $800 from the College and $1,200 from the Department. These allocations should be used for the student’s first two trips before requesting lab travel support.
  • Master’s students: Each Master’s student has one Department travel-support allocation of up to $2,000. This allocation should be used for the student’s first trip before requesting lab travel support.

2. Lab Lodging and Airfare Reimbursement Limits

After the applicable University travel support has been used, the following lab reimbursement caps apply to approved travel.

Travel TypeMaximum Reimbursement CapWhat the Cap Means
Domestic Actual eligible expenses, up to $1,000 (approximately $400 for airfare and $600 for lodging) This is a maximum cap, not a fixed allowance. Reimbursement equals the student’s documented actual allowable expenses and may be less than $1,000.
International Actual eligible expenses, up to $1,500 (approximately $900 for airfare and $600 for lodging) This is a maximum cap, not a fixed allowance. Reimbursement equals the student’s documented actual allowable expenses and may be less than $1,500.

Lodging expectations:

  • Students are expected to share a hotel room whenever reasonably feasible.
  • The lab will generally reimburse only the student’s share of the room cost—normally one-half of the total hotel expense, typically around $600 for four to five nights given discounted conference hotel rates.

Airfare expectations:

  • Students are expected to book flights early to avoid unnecessarily high fares.
  • The lab will reimburse actual airfare expenses, subject to the applicable travel cap.

3. Required Student Travel-Award/Volunteer Applications

When a student is eligible or expected to apply for a conference travel award or volunteer, applying by the stated deadline is a condition of lab reimbursement.

Conference SupportLab Support
Award received The registration fee will be covered by the conference. The lab will reimburse additional meal and transportation expenses up to $250.
Applied, but not awarded The lab will reimburse the conference registration fee.
Did not apply when expected No additional travel expenses will be reimbursed.

4. Reimbursement Procedure

  1. Before requesting lab support, confirm and use any University travel support available to you. PhD students should use their two University travel-support allocations for their first two trips; Master’s students should use their one University travel-support allocation for their first trip.
  2. Before travel, apply for any student travel award for which you are eligible or expected to apply. Request any required letter of support from the PI early enough to meet the award deadline.
  3. After receiving the conference notification, forward the notification email to the PI and confirm whether you plan to attend the conference.
  4. If airfare is substantially higher than the typical amount shown above, request approval from the PI before booking.
  5. The PI will email Kisa to confirm the available budget and reimbursement details. Keep this email thread, as it will be used to process the reimbursement.
  6. After completing the travel, reply to the same email thread and submit all itemized receipts and other required documentation, copying the PI on the email.
Important: All support is reimbursement for actual, allowable expenses supported by itemized receipts. The stated amounts are maximum caps and do not guarantee payment of the full amount. The additional $250 support is added only when the registration fee is waived.
